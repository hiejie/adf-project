Privacy Policy

Note to instructor: this is a draft/mockup prepared for A Day with the Foxes (ADF2027), written in plain language based on what the registration system actually collects and stores. It references the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), since HAU and its students are based in the Philippines. It is not legal advice.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information A Day with the Foxes (ADF2027), an event organized by the Holy Angel University (HAU) School of Computing Student Council, collects when you use this website or register for the event, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following personal information directly from you:

  • Event registration: full name, email address, HAU student number, and a screenshot of your Campus++ student profile (used solely to verify you're a legitimate enrolled student).
  • Contact form: full name, email address, subject, and the content of your message.

We do not collect payment information (the event is free), and the site does not require you to create an account or log in to register.

2. Why we collect it

  • To register you for ADF2027 and confirm your attendance.
  • To verify that registrants are legitimate SOC students, using your student number and Campus++ screenshot.
  • To send you a registration confirmation and any necessary event updates.
  • To respond to questions submitted through the Contact Us form.
  • To prepare attendance records for organizers on event day.

3. Legal basis for processing

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), we process your information on the basis of your consent, given when you check the consent box on the registration form, and because processing is necessary to carry out the event you are registering for.

4. How your information is stored and protected

  • Registration and contact-form records are stored in a database that is not publicly accessible.
  • Campus++ screenshots are encrypted before being saved to disk and can only be viewed by an authenticated organizer through the admin dashboard.
  • Access to registrant data and screenshots is restricted to authorized event organizers/admins, who sign in with a password.
  • We apply reasonable technical safeguards (encryption, access controls, rate-limiting against automated abuse) appropriate for the scale of a student-run event, but no online system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

5. How long we keep your information

We retain registration data, including screenshots, only for as long as necessary to run the event and finalize attendance records — no longer than 90 Days after Event Conclusion — after which it is permanently deleted. Contact-form messages are kept only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information may be shared internally with SOC faculty coordinators solely for event coordination, or disclosed if required by law or a valid request from Holy Angel University or a government authority.

7. Your rights

Under RA 10173, you have the right to:

  • Be informed that your personal information is being processed (this policy).
  • Access your own personal information that we hold.
  • Object to or withdraw consent for further processing, correct inaccurate data, or request that it be deleted, subject to legitimate business/legal reasons for retaining it.
  • Lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission if you believe your data has been mishandled.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies and site usage

The public-facing pages of this site do not use tracking or advertising cookies. A single session cookie is used only on the admin dashboard, to keep an authorized organizer signed in — it is not set for ordinary site visitors or registrants.

9. Children's privacy

This site is intended for use by HAU college students and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 outside the context of ordinary university enrollment.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to socstudentcouncil@gmail.com, or via our Contact Us page.
