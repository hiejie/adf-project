About the Event

A Day with the Foxes (ADF2027) is an annual event in the School of Computing where student organizations showcase their missions, projects, and activities.

Through this event, students can explore organization booths, participate in interactive activities, learn about various fields in computing, and experience the vibrant community that makes the School of Computing unique.

Contact: Dr. Mary Jane Rabena or Sir Bon Flores for more information.
