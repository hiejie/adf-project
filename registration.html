Need help?

How do I get my Campus++ Profile?

Open Campus++, sign in with your student account, and capture the profile page showing your name and student number.

What if I lost my Student ID?

Please contact the School of Computing office before registering so we can help verify your student details.

Can I register on the day itself?

On-site registration may be available while slots last. Register online ahead of time to reserve your place.
